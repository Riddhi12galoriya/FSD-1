* Text-Color:
Text-primary prints blue color.
Text-secondary prints gray color.
Text-success prints green color.
Text-warning prints yellow/orange color.
Text-danger prints red color.
Text-info prints light blue color.
Text-dark prints black/darkgray color.
Text-light prints light color.
Text-muted prints light gray color.
Text-white prints nothing.

*Typography and Text Utilities:
text-lowercase prints in lowercase letters.
text-uppercase prints in uppercase letters.
text-capitalize prints all the first characters of word as capital.
text-decoration-none is used to remove underline.
text-decoration-underline is used to underline the characters.
text-decoration-line-through is used to cut the characters.
text-start is used to print at start of page.
text-end is used to print at end of page.
text-center is used to print at center of page.

fs-1 fw-bold prints as bold and h1.
fs-2 fw-bolder prints as bolder and h2.
fs-3 fw-light prints as light and h3.
fs-4 fw-lighter prints as lighter and h4.
fs-5 fw-italic prints as italic and h5.
fs-6 fw-normal prints as normal and h6.
shadow prints shadow over characters.
shadow-sm prints small shadow.
shadow-lg prints large shadow.
